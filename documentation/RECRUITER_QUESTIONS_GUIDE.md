## Usage Tips

### Best Practices
1. **Be Specific**: The more specific your question, the better the AI can understand and respond
2. **Use Natural Language**: Ask questions as you would naturally speak
3. **Include Context**: Mention relevant details like locations, timeframes, or specific requirements
4. **Try Variations**: If one phrasing doesn't work, try rephrasing the question

### Common Patterns
- Start with action words: "Show me", "Give me", "Analyze", "Find"
- Include specific terms: "breakdown", "analysis", "insights", "trends"
- Mention scope: "our", "all", "comprehensive", "system-wide"
- Specify categories: "by department", "over time", "by location"

### Examples of Effective Questions
- ✅ "Give me a breakdown of all our open positions by department"
- ✅ "Show me recruitment trends over the last quarter"
- ✅ "Analyze our hiring pipeline efficiency"
